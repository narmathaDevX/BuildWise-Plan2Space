## 2. Public Dataset Survey & Candidate Evaluation

We investigated public repositories (Kaggle, Data.gov.in, Mendeley Data, Zenodo, Hugging Face) for candidate data across four domains:

### Candidate A: Kaggle "Smart Construction Cost Estimation India Data"
- **Source / Platform:** Kaggle (`smart-construction-cost-estimation-india-data-csv`).
- **Rows:** ~3,000 synthetic entries.
- **Attributes:** `Project_ID`, `Project_Type`, `Location`, `Project_Size (sq.ft)`, `Complexity`, `Material_Cost`, `Labor_Cost`, `Duration (days)`, `Total_Cost`.
- **License:** Open / Public Domain.
- **Critical Evaluation:**
  - **Data Origin:** The dataset is generated through random uniform distributions and artificial multipliers (simulated numbers generated by student scripts). It does not represent audited real-world construction expenditures.
  - **Verdict: REJECTED.** Violates the non-negotiable honesty constraints: *"Never train an 'ML' model on data you generated yourself from your own formula and then claim it is learned from real data; never fabricate datasets."*

### Candidate B: Indian Real Estate / Housing Transaction Price Datasets (e.g., Bangalore / Mumbai / Chennai Housing Prices)
- **Source / Platform:** Kaggle (Kaggle House Price Prediction India datasets).
- **Attributes:** Location, BHK, square footage, sale price (Lakhs).
- **Critical Evaluation:**
  - **Target Discrepancy:** These datasets track **property resale and market speculative market transaction prices**, which bundle land cost, developer margins, location speculation, and market demand.
  - **Relevance:** Land market value does not measure the **physical cost of building construction materials and labor**. Using sales prices to predict construction expenditure would mislead users by an order of magnitude (e.g., ₹12,000/sq ft market price vs ₹2,000/sq ft construction cost in central Chennai).
  - **Verdict: REJECTED.**

### Candidate C: Floor Plan Datasets (R-PLAN, HouseGAN, Swiss Dwellings)
- **Source:** Academic repositories (Tsinghua University / ETH Zurich).
- **Target:** Vector / raster room layout segmentation for high-rise apartment units.
- **Critical Evaluation:**
  - Academic layout datasets are almost exclusively composed of Japanese/Chinese or European apartment condominiums with predefined structural shear walls, no independent setbacks, no road-orientation Vastu/NBC 2016 requirements, and no Indian municipal bye-law compliance.
  - Generating conceptual floor plans is fundamentally a **geometric constraint satisfaction problem (CSP)** with strict hard boundaries (setbacks, non-overlapping rooms, envelope containment, egress) rather than a statistical token regression problem.
  - **Verdict: REJECTED for ML; RETAINED for Algorithmic Constraint Solver.**

### Candidate D: Official Schedule of Rates (CPWD Plinth Area Rates & TN PWD SOR)
- **Source:** Central Public Works Department (CPWD) Plinth Area Rates (PAR) and Tamil Nadu Public Works Department (TN PWD) Schedule of Rates (SOR 2024–2026).
- **Nature:** Empirical, legally mandated benchmark schedules formulated by senior executive civil engineers based on current market rates of labor (masons, bar benders, carpenters) and materials (IS-grade cement, Fe 500 TMT steel, sand, aggregate, bricks).
- **Verdict: ADOPTED.** Provides transparent, audited, authoritative cost anchors for Tamil Nadu residential construction.

## 3. Decision Summary & Architecture

| Target Component | Technique Chosen | Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Floor Plan Generation** | **Algorithmic Constraint Engine** (Deterministic CP-SAT / Slicing) | Mathematical guarantee of zero room overlap, strict setback compliance, minimum room area enforcement, and reproducible seeds. |
| **Cost Estimation** | **Transparent Engineering Rate Model** (TN PWD / CPWD Benchmark Tables) | Transparent, auditable, explainable to students and home builders; avoids deceptive synthetic ML claims. |
| **Schedule & Cash Flow** | **Discrete-Event Financial Simulation** (Monthly Cash Burn & Dependency Graph) | Physical capacity caps (curing times, stage concurrency) and financial liquidity constraints cannot be learned from static tables. |
| **Machine Learning Status** | **Transparent Formula Mode** (`/api/ml/status` returning `status: "transparent_formula"`) | Full compliance with honesty requirements; no fake models, no synthetic weights. |

## 4. What Dataset Would Be Needed for Real ML?

To justify a supervised machine learning regression model in the future, the following dataset must be curated:
1. **Sample Size:** At least 500+ audited completion records of individual residential buildings built between 2023 and 2026 across Tamil Nadu districts (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Salem, Tirunelveli).
2. **Ground Truth Attributes:**
   - Exact gross plinth built-up area (sq ft) and footprint area.
   - Number of floors and structural type (load-bearing vs RCC framed structure).
   - Soil stratum type and foundation depth (isolated footing vs raft vs pile).
   - Quality specification tier (Economy: red brick/vitrified; Standard: wire-cut/granite; Premium: Italian marble/teak).
   - Actual completion cost (itemized bills verified by chartered civil engineers).
3. **Data Verification:** Official audited tax invoices or bank construction loan disbursement certificates, ensuring real financial ground truth.
